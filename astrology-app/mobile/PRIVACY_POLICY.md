# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 17, 2025  
**Last Updated:** November 17, 2025

## 1. Introduction

Astrology Super App ("App," "we," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process your personal information through our mobile application and related services.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Registration:** Name, email, password, birth date, birth time, birth location
- **Profile Data:** Birth chart details, astrological signs, personal preferences
- **Payment Information:** Processed securely through Stripe; we don't store full credit card details
- **Communications:** Messages to our support team, in-app chat history
- **Subscription Data:** Plan type, billing address, transaction history

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information:** Device type, model, OS version, unique device identifier
- **Usage Analytics:** Features accessed, session duration, interaction patterns
- **Location Data:** Time zone and approximate location (with permission)
- **Health Data:** Optional integration with Apple HealthKit for biorhythm correlation
- **Crash Data:** Error logs, stack traces, performance metrics
- **Cookies & Tracking:** Analytics cookies, session identifiers

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Service Delivery:** Provide personalized horoscopes, birth chart analysis, and AI insights
- **Account Management:** Authenticate users, manage subscriptions, process payments
- **Communication:** Send notifications, updates, support responses
- **Analytics & Improvement:** Analyze usage patterns, optimize features, fix bugs
- **Legal Compliance:** Fulfill legal obligations, prevent fraud, enforce terms
- **AI Enhancement:** Train and improve Anthropic Claude AI for better astrology insights (anonymized)

- **Incident Response:** Immediate notification if breach occurs
